    About Nivel 9

    What Nivel 9 is and what we do.

    We help research groups, universities and companies find funding and bring science to market.

      levels to measure how close a technology is to market

    • 3

    • 0 €

    The journey we accompany

    From idea to the real world, in three stretches (an indicative grouping of the nine levels).

    1. 1–3

      Research

      The principle is observed and the idea is shown to be able to work.

    2. 4–6

      Development

      It is tested in the lab and a prototype is built.

    3. 7–9

      Market

      A prototype works in a real environment and reaches people.

    ▲ Nivel 9 accompanies you along the whole way.

    ✓How we work

    • First enquiries are free, by email.
    • Every figure has its official source and we correct mistakes people point out.
    • Independent and neutral: no parties, no culprits.
    • Clearly: if something needs more work, we tell you before starting.

    ✕What we don’t do

    • We are not a transfer office or a law firm: we give no legal or tax advice.
    • We don’t guarantee a grant will be awarded: the awarding body always decides.
    • Our information is for guidance and doesn’t replace the official call.

    Why “Nivel 9”

    The technology-readiness scale runs from level 1, when a scientific principle is observed, to level 9, when the technology works in the real world. That journey is our work: supporting science until it reaches people.

    Who is behind it

    Nivel 9 is an independent project. We sign as the Nivel 9 Editorial Team and stand behind what we publish. You can write to us at hola@nivel9.es.
