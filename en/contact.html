    Contact

    Write to us. The first enquiry is free.

    Tell us what you research, where you are and what you need. We reply by email with initial guidance and official sources, with no commitment.

    Write to hola@nivel9.es

    What to include

    • Who you are: research group, university, transfer office or company.
    • What you work on, in a few lines and without jargon.
    • What you need: funding, protecting a result, a partner company…
