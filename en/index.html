    Funding deadlines

    A calendar of national and European calls, with a short explanation of what each one is and its official source. The four nearest deadlines are shown at the top, beside the headline.

    See the full calendar

    • Technology Platforms (PTR2026)

      Spanish State Research Agency (AEI)

      Research groups and centres

      Grants to create and consolidate platforms that bring together all players in a sector (universities, centres, companies and associations) to agree on the applied research that is needed. Total budget: €6 million.

      DeadlineNovember 17, 2026
      Official call (AEI)

    • Knowledge Generation Projects (PID2026)

      Spanish State Research Agency (AEI)

      Research groups

      Funds 3 to 4-year research projects that generate new knowledge. Total budget: €724 million. Applications go through universities, public R&D centres, technology centres and non-profits. Closes at 14:00 (mainland Spain time).

      DeadlineNovember 24, 2026
      Official call (AEI)

    • Juan de la Cierva contracts (JDC2026)

      Spanish State Research Agency (AEI)

      Postdoctoral researchers

      3-year contracts for young researchers with a PhD who want to complete their postdoctoral training at a Spanish centre. Open to those who obtained their PhD between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2026. Up to 500 contracts; €35,700 a year plus a one-off €15,000 for research costs. Candidates complete their application until 26 November and institutions submit it until 3 December (per the AEI listing; confirm in the call).

      DeadlineDecember 3, 2026
      Official call (AEI)

    • Ramón y Cajal contracts (RYC2026)

      Spanish State Research Agency (AEI)

      Researchers, talent attraction

      Attracts and retains researchers with a strong track record at Spanish centres. Offers a 5-year contract (€42,600 a year per contract; minimum gross salary €40,000), a personal project of at least €200,000 in direct costs and an incentive towards a permanent post. Total budget: €240 million. Closes at 14:00 (mainland Spain time).

      DeadlineDecember 10, 2026
      Official call (AEI)

    • ERC Consolidator Grant (ERC-2027-COG)

      European Research Council · Horizon Europe

      Researchers 5 to 15 years after their PhD

      Grant for researchers who already lead their own team and want to consolidate it. Up to €2 million over 5 years, plus up to €1 million optional for specific costs (for example equipment or relocation). Open 5 to 15 years after the PhD.

      DeadlineJanuary 12, 2027
      Official ERC page

    • EIC Accelerator · 1st cut-off (full proposal)

      European Innovation Council · Horizon Europe

      Companies and startups (short proposal submitted earlier, on a rolling basis)

      For small companies and startups with an innovation close to market. Grant of up to €2.5 million (technologies at levels 6 to 8) and possible equity investment of €1 to 10 million (levels 6 to 9). This date is for the full proposal; the short one is sent earlier.

      DeadlineJanuary 20, 2027
      Official EIC page

    • EIC Pathfinder Open

      European Innovation Council · Horizon Europe

      Consortia of at least 3 organisations with early-stage technology

      For very early-stage technologies still in research, with a promising future use. Applied for as a consortium of at least three independent organisations. Up to €4 million; technologies at levels 1 to 4. A second cut-off follows on 16 September 2027.

      DeadlineFebruary 25, 2027
      Official EIC page

    • EIC Accelerator · 2nd cut-off (full proposal)

      European Innovation Council · Horizon Europe

      Companies and startups

      Second cut-off for the full EIC Accelerator proposal (same support: grant of up to €2.5 million and possible investment of €1 to 10 million).

      DeadlineMarch 17, 2027
      Official EIC page

    • EIC Transition

      European Innovation Council · Horizon Europe

      Projects building on EU-funded results (for example, a Pathfinder)

      To take a technology proven in the lab (level 3) to a prototype (level 5 or more). It must build on results from projects already funded by the EU, such as a Pathfinder. Up to €2.5 million.

      DeadlineSeptember 14, 2027
      EIC Work Programme 2027
