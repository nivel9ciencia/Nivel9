    What we do

    We support you from funding to market.

    Three services for research groups, universities and companies. First enquiries are free and by email.

    • 01

      Funding tracking

      We watch European and national calls and flag the ones that fit your group, with their dates, requirements and official source.

      Who it’s for: research groups, universities and companies.

      See the deadline calendar

    • 02

      Transfer consulting

      We explain, without jargon, what level your technology is at and when a patent, a licence, a spin-off or a company collaboration makes sense.

      Who it’s for: anyone with research results who doesn’t know where to start.

      Learn the basics with us

    • 03

      Connect with companies

      Register your group or company in a free pool and we put you in touch with possible partners for joint projects.

      Who it’s for: groups, transfer offices, companies and startups.

      See the pool
